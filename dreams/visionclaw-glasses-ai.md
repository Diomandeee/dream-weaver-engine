# VisionClaw — AI Eyes for the Real World

## Essence
An iOS app connecting iPhone/glasses cameras to Gemini Live for real-time visual AI. Four audio architecture variants tested (echo ducking, dual session, silent mode, ACC integration). The convergence build merges the best of all four. Meta Ray-Ban integration planned for always-on visual context.

## Context
Phase 1 complete — direct gateway connection works. 4 audio-fix builds shipped (8A-8D), each testing a different approach to the echo feedback problem. Build 9 convergence next. The deeper vision: AI that sees what you see, hears what you hear, knows your context.
