# Spore — The Idea Garden App

## Essence
A mobile app that makes idea incubation tangible. Plant seeds (ideas), tend them (add context, make connections), watch them grow through lifecycle stages (germinating → growing → budding → blooming → fruiting). SpriteKit particle effects, haptic feedback, and beautiful garden visualization. The dream garden concept made into a consumer product.

## Context
Full design spec exists. iOS SwiftUI + SpriteKit. Growth algorithm with tendril detection, bloom thresholds, and season mechanics. Background processing keeps the garden alive. CloudKit sync. The app embodies the philosophy that ideas need conditions, not commands — patience over productivity.
